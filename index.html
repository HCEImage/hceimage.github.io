EMV Card Analyzer

Android

An Android developer tool for inspecting supported EMV card applications, contextually decoding APDU and BER-TLV data, browsing application records and captured tags, and keeping local analysis history with notes, exportable diagnostic reports and portable traces. Clear result states and bundled offline references support controlled diagnostics; the bounded analyzer is not a payment kernel.
