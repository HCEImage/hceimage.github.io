Which side are you testing?

Both tools keep diagnostic evidence locally. Neither makes real payments or certifies a card or terminal.

Simulate a card · Android

HCE Test Tool

Present a synthetic Visa, Mastercard, American Express or JCB profile from your phone to an authorized test reader or terminal.

  • Choose a Built-in profile, import one or create a copy.
  • Inspect the APDU exchange, profile checks and saved history.
  • Explore test-security overrides and supported device-CVM scenarios when you need them.

Read a card · Android

EMV Card Analyzer

Use your phone's NFC reader to inspect supported applications on a card or test medium you are authorized to analyze.

  • Review captured applications, decoded tags and diagnostic findings.
  • Follow the APDU timeline without performing a payment.
  • Keep local history and export a readable report or portable trace.
